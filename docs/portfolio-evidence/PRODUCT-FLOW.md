## Anonymous and account behavior

**Shipped in development.** Anonymous visitors can complete a recommendation
and reopen/share the stable result URL. Account-scoped history and durable movie
memory require sign-in. Result feedback is shown only when the fetched record
sets `viewerCanRate`; a shared viewer does not inherit the owner's rating
permission. Evidence: [persistence projection](https://github.com/shchilkin/PopChoice/blob/development/apps/web/src/features/recommendation/persistence.ts),
[movie-memory API](https://github.com/shchilkin/PopChoice/tree/development/apps/web/src/app/api/account/movie-memory),
[auth/recommendation e2e](https://github.com/shchilkin/PopChoice/blob/development/apps/web/e2e/recommendation.spec.ts).

## Shipped same-device mode versus planned rooms

**Shipped in development.** Duo and Group share one device and answer
sequentially. The persisted request contains an array of participant answers.

**Planned.** Room persistence, invite links, independent participant sessions,
readiness state, QR invites, and projector/Kahoot-like presentation are tracked
under [issue #359](https://github.com/shchilkin/PopChoice/issues/359) and child
issues [#467](https://github.com/shchilkin/PopChoice/issues/467)–[#470](https://github.com/shchilkin/PopChoice/issues/470).
They must not be described as current group behavior.
